Each fix was entered as radial and DME from your plates. Its position was then predicted on the rendered plan view, using the plate's own NM scale and magnetic variation (plan views are true-north up). It was then compared with where the plate draws the symbol.

Rules and where they come from
Not yet checked
  • Pass marks are provisional until the KCAA instrument rating skill test tolerances are added.
  • The 70° sector line and ±5° flexibility come from the Doc 8168 figure, which isn't in the free text used here. Check them against your ATPL book.
  • No ForeFlight track logs yet, so the model hasn't been compared with your real flown holds and arcs.
  • Station declination isn't printed on the plates, so each procedure uses the airport's magnetic variation. This only matters when comparing with GPS tracks.
  • Roll rate (5°/s) and heading-mode behaviour are model assumptions, not taken from the AFM.
  • Training use only. Chart data is from cycle 13-2026 and is not for navigation.
